Ask your question

    How to use it well

    1. Ask the question to the whole class first. Don't say a name yet.
    2. Press Start think time. Everyone thinks while the countdown runs.
    3. The name appears at the end. Keep it warm: never use it to catch someone out.
    4. Follow up with “Who can add to that?” to bring in more students.

    Based on the Cold Call technique from Doug Lemov, Teach Like a Champion.
